Delete your Evenstep account

Evenstep hasn't launched yet, so there are no accounts to delete. This page explains how deletion will work.

This page is for Evenstep, made by Wong Shou Hong. You can delete your account in the app, or here without the app.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription, and we can't cancel it for you. Cancel it first:

  • iPhone: Settings > tap your name > Subscriptions.
  • Android: Google Play Store > your profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

Option 1: in the app

Open Evenstep > Settings > Account > Delete account, type DELETE and confirm.

Option 2: without the app

  1. Email vauntz.team@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. We'll reply to confirm it's you. If you've lost access to that email but can still open the app, delete your account there. Otherwise, email us and we'll explain what proof we need. We can't delete an account without proof that it's yours.
  3. We delete your account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What we delete

  • your account, email address and sign-in details;
  • your profile (your name and time zone);
  • your private notes and your blocks;
  • your notification tokens;
  • any unused invite codes.

Your open requests are withdrawn. Swaps, holidays, schedule changes, expenses and recurring expenses still waiting for an answer are marked withdrawn, so the other parent isn't left waiting. They stay in the shared history like other records.

What we keep, and why

  • Shared records stay for the other parent. These are messages with their sent and viewed times, calendar entries and requests, expenses (each receipt photo is deleted about 3 months after it's added, on the date the app shows under it; see the Privacy Policy), recurring expense rules, to-dos, the activity log and children's profiles. They belong to the other parent's record too. Your name on them is replaced with "Former member". We delete them 90 days after both parents' accounts are closed. If no other parent is linked to your family, deleting your account deletes all the family's records within 30 days.
  • Billing records are kept for as long as tax law requires (5 years). Apple and Google keep their own purchase records.
  • Legal holds: we keep data longer only if the law requires it, for example a court order.
  • Backups: server backups are overwritten within 7 days.
  • Support emails you sent us: 24 months. Security logs kept by Supabase, our app's hosting provider: 7 days.
  • We also ask RevenueCat, which tracks subscription status for us, to delete your subscription record.

Before you delete, you may want to save a backup or an expense report in the app.
