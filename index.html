One calm place for two homes.

Evenstep is a co-parenting app for separated parents. You share one custody calendar, agree on expenses, and keep a clear record of what was said.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

What it does

  • Custody calendar. See who has the children each day, plan holidays and ask for swaps that the other parent approves.
  • Shared expenses. Log costs with a receipt photo. The other parent approves each one, and you can make Excel or PDF reports.
  • Messages you can rely on. Every message is time-stamped and can't be edited or deleted. A tone check on your phone helps you keep it calm.
  • To-dos and private notes. Share what needs doing, and keep private notes your co-parent never sees.

One Evenstep Plus subscription covers both parents, with a 7-day free trial.
