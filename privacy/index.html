Summary

  • Who we are: Evenstep is made by Wong Shou Hong, an individual developer in Singapore ("we", "us").
  • What we do with your data: we store what you and your co-parent add, so you both see the same calendar, expenses, messages and to-dos.
  • What we never do: no ads, no analytics, no tracking across other apps, and we never sell your data. The tone check runs only on your phone.
  • Children's profiles hold a first name, a colour and, if you choose, a birthday, a photo and school notes. The app has no fields for health information.
  • Your rights: you can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it. Sent messages can't be changed, and shared records stay for the other parent (section 13). Email vauntz.team@gmail.com.

2. The European Economic Area and the United Kingdom

Evenstep isn't currently offered in the European Economic Area (the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway) or the United Kingdom, so we haven't appointed representatives there. If we start offering it there, we'll name our representatives in this section first.

3. Who this policy covers

  • It covers the Evenstep app and our website.
  • You must be 18 or older to use the app.
  • The app is for parents and guardians. It is not for children.
  • Parents may add information about their children (see sections 4 and 6), but we don't knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

6. Children's information

  • A child's profile holds a first name and a colour. A birthday, a photo and school notes are optional.
  • Only you and your linked co-parent can see them. We don't use them for anything else.
  • We never send them to an AI service or use them for marketing.
  • The app has no fields for health information. If you mention health in a message or a private note, it's kept like the rest of that message or note.

7. Information another parent gives us about you

  • A parent invites you by sending you a code or link through their own messaging app. We don't receive your contact details from them.
  • Things in the shared space may mention you, such as calendar entries, expenses and messages, including entries made before you joined.
  • Before you join, the app shows you who we are and this policy.
  • Unused invite codes expire after 30 days.
  • You can object to how we use information about you, or ask us to delete it, at vauntz.team@gmail.com.

8. The tone check runs on your phone

The "Check tone" helper reads your draft on your phone only. Your text is never sent to us or anyone else for this. Its suggestions are optional, and it makes no decisions about you.

9. Who sees or receives your data

  • Your linked co-parent sees everything shared, including when you viewed their messages and whether the family has Plus. They never see your private notes.
  • Private notes are stored on our servers in a space only your account can open in the app. They are not end-to-end encrypted. Our hosting provider stores them, and we access them only to run or repair the service. Like anything we hold, they could be disclosed if a court or other authority lawfully orders it.
  • Service providers process data for us only to run the app and our website. We only use providers that give your data the same or equal protection as this policy, and we use data processing terms where the provider offers them:

  • If the app moves to a company we set up later, your data moves with it under this policy, and we'll tell you first.
  • No sale: we don't sell or share personal information for advertising.

10. International transfers

  • We are based in Singapore. Your data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt), and some providers are in the United States (section 9).
  • We rely on our providers' data processing terms where they offer them. For data leaving the EU or UK, these include the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK Addendum, or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where a provider is certified.
  • For Singapore, we use our providers' contract terms or, where the PDPA allows, the fact that the transfer is needed to give you the service you asked for.
  • Email us for a copy of these safeguards.

12. Deleting your account

  • In the app: Settings > Account > Delete account.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on our Delete your account page.
  • What we delete:
    • your account and sign-in details;
    • your private notes;
    • your notification tokens;
    • any unused invite codes.
  • What we keep, and why:
    • Shared records (messages with their sent and viewed times, calendar entries and requests, expenses with receipt photos, recurring expense rules, to-dos, the activity log and children's profiles) stay for the other parent (section 11). Your name on them is replaced with "Former member". If no other parent is linked, we delete them within 30 days.
    • Billing records are kept for as long as tax law requires (5 years).
    • We also ask RevenueCat, which tracks subscription status for us, to delete your subscription record.
  • Subscriptions: deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. We can't cancel it for you, so cancel it in the App Store or Google Play first.
  • Export first: save a backup or report before you delete.

13. Your rights

You can:

  • ask for a copy of your data, or have it sent to you in a portable format;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • limit or object to how we use it;
  • withdraw consent at any time.

Sent messages can't be changed. To correct one, send a new message.

How: email vauntz.team@gmail.com from your account email. We'll confirm it's you and reply within one month. Complex requests may take up to two more months, and we'll tell you if so.

Shared records: some data belongs to both parents. We may refuse to delete the other parent's copy where we need it to defend legal claims, or where deleting it would harm the other parent's rights. We'll explain why.

Complaints: you can complain to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission, or to the data protection authority where you live.

14. What you must give us

You need an email address to create an account. Everything else is optional, including children's photos and school notes, and the app still works without them.

15. Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our hosting provider.
  • Access rules mean only the two linked parents can read a family's data.
  • Our own access is limited to running and supporting the service.
  • If a breach happens, we tell the regulators and the people affected as the law requires. That includes parents where children's data is involved.

16. Backup and report files on your phone

  • What's in them: backup files contain your shared records. Private notes are never included.
  • Where they go: you choose where to save them, and we don't receive them.
  • Keep them safe: store them carefully, and only share them with people you trust.

17. United States

  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites. So we don't respond differently to "Do Not Track" signals.
  • No third party collects information about your online activity over time and across other websites through our app or website.
  • We don't sell or share personal information, as California law defines those words.
  • We honour the requests in section 13 from any US state.

18. Changes to this policy

  • The date and version at the top change with every update.
  • Every version is listed under "Changes" at the end of this page. Email us for a copy of an earlier one.
