## Summary

- **Who we are:** Evenstep is made by Wong Shou Hong, an individual developer in Singapore ("we", "us").
- **What we do with your data:** we store what you and your co-parent add, so you both see the same calendar, expenses, messages and to-dos.

- **Your rights:** you can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it. Sent messages can't be changed, and shared records stay for the other parent (section 13). Email vauntz.team@gmail.com.

## 8. The tone check runs on your phone

The "Check tone" helper reads your draft on your phone only. Your text is never sent to us or anyone else for this. Its suggestions are optional, and it makes no decisions about you.

## 12. Deleting your account

- **In the app:** Settings > Account > Delete account.
- **Without the app:** follow the steps on our [Delete your account](/delete-account/) page.
- **What we delete:**
    - your account and sign-in details;
    - your private notes;
    - your notification tokens;
    - any unused invite codes.
- **What we keep, and why:**
    - Shared records (messages with their sent and viewed times, calendar entries and requests, expenses with receipt photos, recurring expense rules, to-dos, the activity log and children's profiles) stay for the other parent (section 11). Your name on them is replaced with "Former member". If no other parent is linked, we delete them within 30 days.
    - Billing records are kept for as long as tax law requires (5 years).
    - We also ask RevenueCat, which tracks subscription status for us, to delete your subscription record.
- **Subscriptions:** deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. We can't cancel it for you, so cancel it in the App Store or Google Play first.
- **Export first:** save a backup or report before you delete.

## 13. Your rights

You can:

- ask for a copy of your data, or have it sent to you in a portable format;
- correct it;
- delete it;
- limit or object to how we use it;
- withdraw consent at any time.

Sent messages can't be changed. To correct one, send a new message.

**How:** email vauntz.team@gmail.com from your account email. We'll confirm it's you and reply within one month. Complex requests may take up to two more months, and we'll tell you if so.

**Early-access list:** to leave it, reply to any of our emails or email us, and we'll delete your sign-up.

**Shared records:** some data belongs to both parents. We may refuse to delete the other parent's copy where we need it to defend legal claims, or where deleting it would harm the other parent's rights. We'll explain why.

**Complaints:** you can complain to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission, or to the data protection authority where you live.
