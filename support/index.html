Support

Evenstep isn't in the App Store or Google Play yet. You can still email us with any question.

Email: vauntz.team@gmail.com. We usually reply within 3 working days.

Delete your account: in the app, Settings > Account > Delete account. Without the app, see Delete your account.

Please tell us your phone type, the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and what happened. Never send passwords or card numbers.

Subscription

What does Evenstep Plus cost?

After a 7-day free trial, it costs the price shown in the App Store or Google Play. In the US that's currently US$3.99 a month or US$29.99 a year. One subscription covers both linked parents.

How do I cancel?

  • iPhone: Settings > tap your name > Subscriptions > Evenstep > Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play Store > your profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Evenstep > Cancel subscription.

Plus continues until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel it, and we can't cancel it for you.

Can I get a refund?

  • App Store purchases: Apple decides refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Google Play purchases: request one in Google Play, or email us.

I paid but don't have Plus.

Tap Restore purchase in Settings > Plan. Use the same Apple or Google account you paid with.

Your co-parent and your records

How do I invite my co-parent?

Settings > Invite your co-parent.

Can I edit or delete a message?

No. Messages are permanent and time-stamped, so both parents can rely on them. To correct one, send a new message.

How do I export my records?

  • Expense reports: Money > Report gives you an Excel or PDF file.
  • Full backup: Settings > Save a backup file saves everything shared. Private notes are left out.

Report a problem with your co-parent

Email us with the date and what happened. We can't take sides in disputes or give legal advice. If anyone is in danger, call your local emergency number.

Legal or records requests

We respond to valid legal requests only. Lawyers and courts should email vauntz.team@gmail.com with "Legal request" in the subject.

Privacy and your account

How is my data used?

See our Privacy Policy. To ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion, email us from your account email.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings > Account > Delete account. Without the app: see Delete your account.
